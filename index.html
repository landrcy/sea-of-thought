    How it works

    Three steps to the horizon

    1. 1 · Write

      Pour up to 280 characters onto a scroll. Add a name if you like, or stay a mystery.

    2. 2 · Seal

      Your scroll is rolled, tied with twine, slid into a glass bottle and corked.

    3. 3 · Drift

      Splash! It joins the sea with its number and time. Click any bottle to unroll it.
